TOWER CRANES AT TAIPEI/TAIWAN TAOYUAN INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT

With effect from 1 January 2023, 0000UTC until 31 August 2024, 1000UTC, tower cranes are erected at Taipei/Taiwan Taoyuan International Airport. The related information is as follows:
  1. Information of the tower cranes (related location is as attached graphic):
    1. Construction area :
      250420N 1211326E
      250430N 1211338E
      250424N 1211345E
      250413N 1211332E
    2. Elevation: 82.5M AMSL
    3. Operational hours: Daily 0000-1000UTC and weather condition reported at Taipei/Taiwan Taoyuan International airport is VMC.
    4. Obstacle lights: Top of the tower cranes are equipped with low-intensity type A obstacle lights.
  2. Remarks:
    1. During the operational hours, RCTP IAP ILS RWY23R (CAT II) is suspended.
    2. Any change to work period and elevation of tower cranes of this AIP SUP will be notified by NOTAM.
